## Question
For answering questions over a large internal handbook, should we use RAG or fine-tuning?

## Sources considered
- rag-study-a: internal study comparing RAG and fine-tuning on a 4,000-page handbook.
- rag-study-b: engineering blog on a support bot with a small fine-tuned model.
- finetune-cost-note: cost and operations note, no quality comparison.

## Findings
### Agreed across sources
- Fine-tuning needs retraining when the knowledge changes (rag-study-a, rag-study-b).
### Disagreements
- rag-study-a finds RAG more accurate (81% vs 64%); rag-study-b finds similar factual accuracy (about 75%) and better style with fine-tuning.
### Gaps (not covered by any source)
- No source measured latency or tested non-English documents.

## Risks and unknowns
- rag-study-b only tested documents up to 300 pages, much smaller than our handbook (inference).

## Recommended next steps
- Run both approaches on 50 real handbook questions and compare accuracy.
- Measure retrieval latency on the full handbook before choosing.
